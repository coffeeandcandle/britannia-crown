Key Stage 2 SATs results are normally **released to schools in early July** and shared with families before the end of the summer term.

<Callout title="At a glance">

- Results are **scaled scores** from 80 to 120, with **100** meaning the expected standard.
- Writing is **assessed by teachers**.
- Share the results with your child's **secondary school**.

</Callout>

## What you will receive

Parents usually receive a report showing scaled scores in reading and maths, the grammar, punctuation and spelling result and the teacher assessment for writing.

## How to read them

A score of 100 or more means the expected standard has been met. See [SATs scaled scores explained](/guides/sats-scaled-scores-explained).

## What to do next

Use the results as a snapshot and note any areas to support over the summer. Pass them to the secondary school if they have not already been shared. See [moving from Year 6 to Year 7](/guides/moving-from-year-6-to-year-7).

Check dates with your child's school, as release dates are set each year.
