For students who applied by the October deadline, the autumn term brings admissions tests, and for those shortlisted, interviews, often in December. Dates and formats vary by course and college.

<Callout title="At a glance">

- Interviews are **academic conversations**, not trick questions.
- Many students are asked to **think aloud** about unfamiliar problems.
- Interviews may be online or in person. Check your invitation.

</Callout>

## How to help

- Encourage your child to **practise talking through ideas** with teachers or friends.
- Help with **practicalities**: travel, accommodation and timings.
- Keep a calm routine with good sleep and meals.
- Remind them that an invitation is already an achievement.

## What not to do

Avoid last-minute cramming or repeated mock interviews that cause stress. Keep perspective: outcomes depend on many factors.

## After the interview

Offers are usually released in January. Encourage your child to focus on A-Level preparation in the meantime. See [Oxbridge applications: what parents can do](/guides/oxbridge-applications-what-parents-can-do).
