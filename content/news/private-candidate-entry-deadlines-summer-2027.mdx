Private candidates, including home educated students and adults, must find an exam centre and be entered before deadlines that often fall in the winter or early spring. Exam centres can have earlier deadlines of their own.

<Callout title="At a glance">

- **Contact centres early**; places are limited.
- Check **subjects, boards and fees**.
- Confirm **deadlines** in writing.

</Callout>

## What to do now

- Decide on subjects and exam boards. See [GCSE exam boards](/guides/gcse-exam-boards-aqa-edexcel-ocr).
- Call local centres that accept private candidates.
- Ask about coursework and speaking assessments, which can limit options.

See [entering GCSEs as a private candidate](/guides/private-candidate-gcse-how-to-enter).
