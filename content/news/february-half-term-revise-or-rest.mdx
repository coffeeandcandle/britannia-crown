February half term falls in the middle of the exam year. The answer is usually **a bit of both**: some structured revision and real rest.

<Callout title="At a glance">

- Plan **two or three short sessions** a day, not long days.
- Protect **rest and sleep**.
- Use the week to act on **mock exam feedback**.

</Callout>

## A sensible balance

Students who revise in short, focused blocks and take real breaks tend to retain more than those who work all day. A few hours of work with time outside is plenty.

## What to do

- Review mock feedback and pick topics.
- Practise a few exam questions.
- Spend time on friends, exercise and hobbies.

## Parents' role

Encourage, don't nag. Ask what the plan is and offer help. See [how to support a child through exam stress](/guides/how-to-support-a-child-through-exam-stress) and [how to build a revision timetable](/guides/how-to-build-a-revision-timetable).
