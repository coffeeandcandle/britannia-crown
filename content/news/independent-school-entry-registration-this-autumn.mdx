Independent schools set their own admissions timelines, and for many the autumn and winter are busy, with open events, registration deadlines and assessments for entry at 7, 8, 11 and 13.

<Callout title="At a glance">

- Check **each school's dates**; they vary.
- Register **early**, as some schools ask years ahead.
- Ask about **scholarships and bursaries** and their deadlines.

</Callout>

## What to do now

- Make a list of schools and their key dates.
- Book open days and visits.
- Register your child where required.
- Plan for assessments, such as pre-tests and interviews. See [independent school entry](/guides/independent-school-entry-assessments-and-bursaries).

## Different ages, different routes

Entry at 7 or 8 often involves gentle assessments. See [7+ and 8+ entry](/guides/seven-plus-and-eight-plus-entry). Entry at 13 may involve Common Entrance or pre-tests. See [Common Entrance and prep schools explained](/guides/common-entrance-and-prep-schools-explained).
