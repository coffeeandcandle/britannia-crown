The spring term runs straight into exam season, so planning tutoring **before it starts** gives your child the longest runway.

<Callout title="At a glance">

- Decide **goals first**, such as a topic, grade or exam.
- Choose a **regular weekly slot** that fits the week.
- **Review progress** at half term.

</Callout>

## Why start early

Early planning lets a tutor find gaps, build confidence and work towards exams steadily, rather than rushing in the final weeks.

## What to decide

- **Subject and goal.** What does your child need to improve?
- **Frequency.** See [how often a child should have tutoring](/guides/how-often-should-a-child-have-tutoring).
- **Format.** Online or face to face.
- **Budget.** See [how much a tutor costs](/guides/how-much-does-a-private-tutor-cost-uk).
