UK and Dubai school terms **do not match exactly**. Families moving between them should check dates with the schools involved.

<Callout title="At a glance">

- Dubai schools publish **their own term dates**, and they vary by curriculum.
- The **year-group cut-off** can differ from England's.
- Plan any move around **term starts** and exam dates.

</Callout>

## Why dates differ

Schools in Dubai set term dates under local rules, so the start and end of terms, and holidays, differ from England. British-curriculum schools follow their own calendars.

## What to check

- Term and holiday dates for the school.
- Which **year group** your child will join.
- Whether your child is **mid-way through exam courses**.
