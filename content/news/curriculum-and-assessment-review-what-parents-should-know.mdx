The Curriculum and Assessment Review looked at what children learn and how they are assessed in England, and set out recommendations for change. Recommendations are not the same as decisions, and implementation takes time, so check gov.uk for what has actually been agreed and when.

<Callout title="At a glance">

- The review made **recommendations** about curriculum and assessment.
- Changes take **years** to reach classrooms.
- For now, your child's **current courses and exams** stay the same unless told otherwise.

</Callout>

## What it covered

The review considered areas such as the balance of the curriculum, how much of GCSE assessment relies on exams and how to track pupils' progress. It sets out a direction of travel, not a timetable for any single child.

## What to do

- Do not change plans for your child based on proposals.
- Ask your child's school how changes might affect their year group.
- Follow official updates from the Department for Education.

## Why it matters

Changes could affect GCSE courses and assessment over the coming years. For now, see [GCSE grades explained](/guides/gcse-grades-explained) and [Key Stage 3 explained](/guides/key-stage-3-explained) for the current system.
