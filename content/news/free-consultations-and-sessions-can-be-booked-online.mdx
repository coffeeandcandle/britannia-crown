Booking with Britannia Crown is now simpler. You can choose a free 30-minute consultation, a paid strategy session or a trial tutoring lesson, and pick a time online.

<Callout title="At a glance">

- **Free consultation:** 30 minutes with an education consultant.
- **Strategy session:** a longer 60-minute session with a written summary.
- **Trial lesson:** a first one-to-one lesson with a matched tutor.

</Callout>

## How it works

Visit our [booking page](/book-consultation), choose the option that suits you, then pick from the available times. We confirm by email.

## Which should you choose?

Start with the free consultation if you are not sure what you need. A strategy session suits families with a specific decision to make, such as a school choice or exam plan. A trial lesson lets your child meet a tutor before committing.
