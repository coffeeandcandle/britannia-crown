Each autumn, exam boards run a November series of GCSE English language and maths for students who still need a grade 4. If your child is resitting this month, a few facts help.

<Callout title="At a glance">

- November is for **English language and maths** only.
- It is aimed at students **aged 16 and over** who did not reach grade 4.
- Entries are made by **schools and colleges**, and results arrive around the new year.

</Callout>

## Who sits them

Students who missed a grade 4 in the summer and are in post-16 education can resit. They generally need to keep studying maths and English until they reach it. See [GCSE resits in English and maths](/guides/gcse-resits-english-and-maths).

## Preparing

If your child is sitting now, focus on targeted revision using past papers and mark schemes. Encourage regular sleep and good routines. A tutor can help pinpoint where marks were lost.

## Results

Results are usually published in January. If a grade is close, ask the school about the options. See [reviews of marking and exam appeals](/guides/exam-reviews-of-marking-and-appeals).

## If the November series is missed

The next opportunity is usually the summer series. Speak to the college about plans.
