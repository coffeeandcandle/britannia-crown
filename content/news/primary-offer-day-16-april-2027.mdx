National offer day for primary school places in England falls on **16 April**. In 2027 that is a Friday, so offers should arrive that day.

<Callout title="At a glance">

- You will receive **one offer** from your council.
- **Accept by the deadline** in the letter.
- You can ask for **waiting lists** and **appeal**.

</Callout>

## What happens

Your council allocates places using each school's admission criteria and sends an offer. You will be told how to accept and by when.

## If you are happy

Accept the place. Start planning visits, uniform and transition days. See [starting Reception: what to expect](/guides/starting-reception-what-to-expect).

## If you are disappointed

Accept the place offered to keep it. Then ask to join waiting lists for schools you prefer. Waiting lists are ordered by admission criteria, not by date, so positions can move.

## Infant class size appeals

Appeals are harder for Reception, Year 1 and Year 2 because of the limit on class sizes. See [school admission appeals in England](/guides/school-admission-appeals-england).

## Preparing your child

Talk about school in positive terms, and read stories about starting school together.
