Before moving abroad, **collect your child's school records**. New schools usually ask for them during admissions.

<Callout title="At a glance">

- Ask the current school for **recent reports and assessment results**.
- Collect **exam entries and results** for older children.
- Keep **copies** in case a new school asks again.

</Callout>

## What to collect

- Recent school reports, ideally the last two years.
- Test or assessment results, such as SATs or mock grades.
- Details of exam courses, boards and entries.
- Any information about special educational needs.

## Why it matters

Records help a new school place your child in the right year group and plan support. See [international pupils applying to UK schools](/guides/international-pupils-applying-to-uk-schools).
