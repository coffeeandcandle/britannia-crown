A-Level results come out in mid-August, and GCSE results about a week later. Exact dates are published by the exam boards each year.

<Callout title="At a glance">

- Have a **Plan A and Plan B** before results day.
- Know your **school and university contact details**.
- Be around on the day, in case you need to make decisions.

</Callout>

## Before results day

- Check which offers or places depend on results.
- Note deadlines for responding.
- Know who to call at the school, sixth form or university.

## On the day

See [results day: what to do](/guides/results-day-gcse-a-level-what-to-do). If a result is a surprise, ask about options such as [reviews of marking](/guides/exam-reviews-of-marking-and-appeals) or [retaking](/guides/retaking-a-levels).
