In England, schools are generally not allowed to authorise holidays in term time except in exceptional circumstances. Councils can issue penalty notices for unauthorised absence, under a national framework.

<Callout title="At a glance">

- Holidays in term time are **normally unauthorised**.
- Penalty notices are issued **under a national framework**; amounts are set nationally.
- Ask your school and council about **exceptional circumstances**.

</Callout>

## How fines work

Under the national framework, a fine for unauthorised absence can be issued per parent, per child, with a lower amount if paid quickly and a higher one if paid later. Check your council for the current amounts and rules.

## What counts as exceptional

Schools decide on a case-by-case basis. Examples might include a bereavement or a family emergency. Holidays are not usually accepted.

## Practical tips

- Check the school's term dates before booking.
- Speak to the school early if you think your circumstances are exceptional.
- Keep records of any communication.
