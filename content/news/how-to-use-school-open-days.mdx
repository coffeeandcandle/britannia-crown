Open days are a good way to see a school **in action**. A little preparation helps you compare schools fairly.

<Callout title="At a glance">

- Prepare a **list of questions** before you go.
- Look at **how pupils and staff behave**, not only the displays.
- Check the **admissions criteria** separately.

</Callout>

## Before you go

Read the school's website and its latest inspection report, and note anything you want to ask.

## What to look for

- How pupils speak to staff and each other.
- How classrooms feel and what work is on the walls.
- How the school handles behaviour and support.

## Questions to ask

See [questions to ask when visiting a school](/guides/questions-to-ask-when-visiting-a-school).

## After the visit

Compare notes with your child. Remember that visiting a school does not affect your chances of a place, which are set by the criteria. See [how school admissions criteria work](/guides/how-school-admissions-criteria-work), [choosing a secondary school](/guides/how-to-choose-a-secondary-school) and [choosing a primary school](/guides/how-to-choose-a-primary-school).
