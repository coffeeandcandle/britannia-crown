Exam stress tends to build in the spring. Small, steady habits help students stay well.

<Callout title="At a glance">

- Protect **sleep, meals and exercise**.
- Keep **routines calm and predictable**.
- Talk about **effort and progress**, not only grades.

</Callout>

## Habits that help

- Regular bedtimes, especially before exams.
- Breaks and fresh air between study sessions.
- A balanced timetable with rest days. See [how to build a revision timetable](/guides/how-to-build-a-revision-timetable).

## What parents can do

Ask how your child is feeling and listen. Avoid comparing them with others. See [how to support a child through exam stress](/guides/how-to-support-a-child-through-exam-stress).

## When to seek help

If worries are affecting sleep, eating or mood for a long time, speak to the school and your GP.

For exam-day advice, read [what to do on exam day](/guides/what-to-do-on-exam-day).
