Easter is the **last long break** before the summer exams. A balanced plan can make it count without wearing students out.

<Callout title="At a glance">

- Aim for **a few focused hours a day**, not full days.
- Mix **new revision with past papers**.
- Keep **rest days** and time outdoors.

</Callout>

## A balanced plan

- Spend mornings on the **hardest subjects**.
- Use afternoons for **lighter tasks** such as flashcards.
- Include a **full rest day** each week.

## Focus on what matters

Use recent mock results and teacher feedback to choose topics. See [how to use past papers](/guides/how-to-use-past-papers).

## Keeping it sustainable

Sleep, meals and breaks help students retain what they revise. See [how to support a child through exam stress](/guides/how-to-support-a-child-through-exam-stress) and [how to build a revision timetable](/guides/how-to-build-a-revision-timetable).
