Most GCSE and A-Level exams are sat between May and June. Exam boards and schools publish timetables well in advance, so check yours as soon as it is available.

<Callout title="At a glance">

- The main season is **May to June** each year.
- Timetables are published by the **exam boards** and **your school**.
- Early, steady revision is **calmer** than late cramming.

</Callout>

## What to do now

- Ask the school for your child's entries and exam boards.
- Build a rough plan with milestones. See [how to build a revision timetable](/guides/how-to-build-a-revision-timetable).
- Use mocks to identify gaps. See [GCSE mock exams](/guides/gcse-mock-exams-how-to-use-them).
