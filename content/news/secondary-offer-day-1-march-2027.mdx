National offer day for secondary school places in England is **1 March**, and in 2027 that falls on a Monday. Most families will receive an email or letter from their council on that day.

<Callout title="At a glance">

- You will be offered **one school**, the highest-ranked of your preferences that could be offered.
- **Accept the place** by the council's deadline.
- You can join **waiting lists** and **appeal**.

</Callout>

## What to expect

Councils usually send offers by email in the morning, and some by post. The offer will tell you the school, how to accept and the deadline for responding.

## If you are offered your first choice

Accept by the deadline. Tell the other schools you will not need their places, if the council asks.

## If you are not

You will still be offered a place, often at a school lower on your list. You can accept it, join waiting lists for higher preferences and consider an appeal. See [what if you don't get your first choice school](/guides/what-if-you-dont-get-your-first-choice-school).

## Appeals

You normally have at least 20 school days from the date of the refusal letter to lodge an appeal. See [school admission appeals in England](/guides/school-admission-appeals-england).

## Looking after your child

Be thoughtful about how you talk about offers, since children often pick up on parents' anxiety.
