For students in Year 11, the autumn term is the time to research and apply for post-16 courses. Deadlines vary, so check each provider.

<Callout title="At a glance">

- Applications often **open in the autumn** and close in the winter.
- Offers are usually **conditional on GCSE results**.
- Apply to **more than one** provider.

</Callout>

## What to do now

- Visit open days and compare courses. See [how to choose a sixth form or college](/guides/how-to-choose-a-sixth-form-or-college).
- Check **entry requirements**, including grades needed for each course.
- Ask the school about predicted grades. See [predicted grades explained](/guides/predicted-grades-explained).
- Apply on time, and keep a backup.

## Consider the options

A-Levels, BTECs, T Levels and apprenticeships all lead to different routes. See [BTEC or A-Level](/guides/btec-vs-a-level) and [apprenticeships after GCSE](/guides/apprenticeships-after-gcse).

## After applying

Attend interviews or taster days if invited, and keep working on GCSE preparation.
