Many independent schools hold **entrance assessments in the autumn and winter** before the year of entry. Dates and formats vary, so check each school.

<Callout title="At a glance">

- Schools set their own **dates, formats and fees**.
- Assessments often include **English, maths and reasoning**, sometimes an interview.
- **Calm, light preparation** works better than intense cramming.

</Callout>

## What to expect

Younger children may have classroom-style activities, older children formal papers and an interview. See [entry assessments and bursaries](/guides/independent-school-entry-assessments-and-bursaries).

## How to prepare

Keep preparation relaxed with reading, number practice and a few sample questions. See [7+ and 8+ entry](/guides/seven-plus-and-eight-plus-entry).

## Check each school

Ask each school for the dates, format and registration deadline. See [how to apply to an independent school](/guides/how-to-apply-to-an-independent-school).
