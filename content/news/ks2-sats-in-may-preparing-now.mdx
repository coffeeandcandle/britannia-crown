Key Stage 2 SATs take place in the spring term each year, in May. Children are tested in reading, maths and grammar, punctuation and spelling. Dates are published by the Department for Education.

<Callout title="At a glance">

- SATs are **taken in May** in school.
- Results are reported as **scaled scores**.
- They are about **school accountability** and secondary transition, not your child's worth.

</Callout>

## Winter and spring

- Keep up regular reading, times tables and spelling practice.
- Ask the teacher where your child could use support.
- Keep sleep and play a priority.

## Avoiding pressure

Short, regular practice beats heavy last-minute revision. Praise effort. See [KS2 SATs explained](/guides/ks2-sats-explained).

## Skills worth building

- Reading comprehension, with talk about books.
- Number fluency. See [how to help your child with maths at home](/guides/how-to-help-your-child-with-maths-at-home).
- Grammar and spelling. See [Year 6 grammar, punctuation and spelling](/guides/year-6-grammar-punctuation-and-spelling-explained).
