If your child was born between 1 September 2022 and 31 August 2023, they are due to start Reception in September 2027, and the national closing date for applications is **15 January 2027**.

<Callout title="At a glance">

- Closing date: **15 January 2027**.
- Offers: **16 April 2027**, the national offer day.
- Apply through your **home local authority**, even for schools in other areas.

</Callout>

## What to do now

- Look at the schools near you and read each school's admission policy.
- Visit open days and tours, which often run in the autumn.
- Check how places were offered last year to judge your chances.
- List your preferences in order, including at least one school you are confident of getting.

See [how to choose a primary school](/guides/how-to-choose-a-primary-school) for questions to ask.

## Summer-born children

If your child was born between April and August and you are thinking about a delayed start, make the request alongside your application. See [summer-born children and starting school](/guides/summer-born-children-and-starting-school).
