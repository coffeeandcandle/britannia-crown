Our Ambassador Programme rewards people who recommend Britannia Crown to families.

<Callout title="At a glance">

- **£30** for every successful referral.
- **No cap** on how many families you refer.
- A referral is successful when a family uses your code and completes a **paid booking**.

</Callout>

## Who it is for

Nannies, au pairs, teachers, educators, parents and others who talk to families about education.

## How it works

Sign up, get your personal code, share it, and when a family uses it and books a paid session, you are paid by bank transfer. Full details and questions are on the [Ambassador page](/ambassador).
