For Year 11 students, the spring term is where revision gets serious. A **simple, steady plan** works better than last-minute cramming.

<Callout title="At a glance">

- Start with **mock exam feedback** to choose priorities.
- Plan **short daily sessions** across subjects.
- Add **past papers** as the term goes on.

</Callout>

## A simple plan

1. **Pick priorities** from mock results.
2. **Make a timetable** with short sessions and rest days. See [how to build a revision timetable](/guides/how-to-build-a-revision-timetable).
3. **Use active methods** such as flashcards and practice questions.
4. **Practise past papers** under timed conditions. See [how to use past papers](/guides/how-to-use-past-papers).

## Looking after wellbeing

Sleep, food and breaks all affect performance. See [supporting a child through exam stress](/guides/how-to-support-a-child-through-exam-stress).
