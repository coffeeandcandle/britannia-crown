For most undergraduate courses, the main UCAS deadline falls in mid-January. Check the UCAS website for the exact date and time for 2027 entry, because deadlines are set precisely.

<Callout title="At a glance">

- The **main deadline is in mid-January**; earlier for Oxford, Cambridge, medicine, dentistry and veterinary courses.
- Students can apply for **up to five courses**.
- Schools usually set an **earlier internal deadline** for references.

</Callout>

## What to do now

- Finish the **personal statement** and ask a teacher to read it. See [UCAS personal statement: a parent's guide](/guides/ucas-personal-statement-guide-for-parents).
- Check **predicted grades** with the school. See [predicted grades explained](/guides/predicted-grades-explained).
- Make sure **course choices** match entry requirements. See [A-Level grades and UCAS points explained](/guides/a-level-grades-and-ucas-points-explained).
- Agree who will pay the application fee and check the personal details.

## After applying

Universities respond over the following months. Students reply to offers by the dates UCAS sets.
