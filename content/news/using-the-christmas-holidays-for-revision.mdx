The Christmas break is a useful stretch of time for GCSE and A-Level students, but they also need rest. A light, regular plan is better than long days of revision.

<Callout title="At a glance">

- Aim for **short daily sessions** on most days.
- Take several **full days off**, including around Christmas itself.
- Focus on **weaker topics** and past paper questions.

</Callout>

## A simple plan

- Two or three sessions of 45 minutes on revision days.
- One subject focus per session, such as 'fractions' or 'a poem'.
- A short review at the end of each day.

## Make it realistic

Build the plan with your child, so they own it. Include time with family and friends. See [how to build a revision timetable](/guides/how-to-build-a-revision-timetable).

## Use past papers sensibly

Use a couple of full papers under timed conditions to check progress, and mark them carefully. See [how to use past papers well](/guides/how-to-use-past-papers).

## Rest matters

Sleep and downtime help memory and mood. See [how to support a child through exam stress](/guides/how-to-support-a-child-through-exam-stress).
