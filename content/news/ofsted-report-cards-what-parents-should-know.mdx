Ofsted has been changing how it reports on schools in England, replacing single overall judgments with report cards that grade several areas separately. The details and rollout can change, so check Ofsted's website for the school you are looking at.

<Callout title="At a glance">

- Newer reports can show **several separate grades**.
- **Older reports** still use the earlier overall labels.
- Check the **inspection date** and format.

</Callout>

## What it means for parents

You may see schools with different types of reports side by side. Do not compare an older single label with a newer report card as if they were the same. Read the detail in each.

## How to use them

- Look at the **areas** that matter most to you, such as behaviour or support for pupils with additional needs.
- Check the **date** of the inspection.
- Combine reports with visits and conversations.

See [Ofsted ratings explained for parents](/guides/ofsted-ratings-explained).

## A note of caution

Inspection systems change over time. For the latest information, check Ofsted's own guidance.
