If your child was born between 1 April and 31 August 2023, they are due to start Reception in September 2027. Parents of summer-born children can ask for entry to be delayed by a year, and the request is generally made as part of the normal application round.

<Callout title="At a glance">

- Apply by **15 January 2027** and make your request at the same time.
- Provide **reasons and evidence**.
- Councils or schools decide, so start early.

</Callout>

## Why act now

Admission authorities may have their own forms or processes for these requests. Starting in the autumn gives you time to gather support from nursery staff or other professionals.

## What to include

Explain why you believe a delayed start is best for your child. Include observations, reports or professional advice.

## What happens next

If the request is agreed, your child starts Reception a year later and you normally need to apply again in the following year's round. If it is refused, you can still accept a place in the normal year group. Check your council's process.

## Think it through

Talk to your child's nursery and the schools you are considering. See [summer-born children and starting school](/guides/summer-born-children-and-starting-school).
