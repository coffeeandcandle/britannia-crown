Parents' evenings are often only a few minutes long, so a short list of questions helps you get the most from them.

<Callout title="At a glance">

- Ask about **progress, effort and next steps**.
- Ask what **you can do at home**.
- Follow up in writing if something needs action.

</Callout>

## Ten questions to ask

1. How is my child doing compared with what you expect for their age?
2. What are their strengths?
3. Where do they find work hardest?
4. How is their effort and behaviour in class?
5. Are there any gaps in earlier learning?
6. What are the next steps for them?
7. How can I help at home?
8. How are friendships and wellbeing?
9. Is there any extra support available in school?
10. When will we next hear about progress?

## Before you go

Ask your child what they would like you to raise. Bring recent reports or work.

## After the meeting

Write down actions and follow up with the school if needed. If gaps emerge, a tutor may help. See [signs your child needs a tutor](/guides/signs-your-child-needs-a-tutor).
