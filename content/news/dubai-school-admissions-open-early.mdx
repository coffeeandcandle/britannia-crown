Popular British-curriculum schools in Dubai can have **waiting lists**, so starting early gives families more choice.

<Callout title="At a glance">

- Some schools **fill early**, especially in popular year groups.
- Registration is often **open well before term**.
- Check **ratings, fees and curriculum** for each school.

</Callout>

## Why early matters

In-demand schools may take registrations months before the academic year, and some year groups are full quickly. Starting early lets you join waiting lists and compare alternatives.

## What to do

- Make a shortlist and check each school's registration process.
- Look at ratings. See [KHDA ratings explained](/guides/khda-ratings-explained).
- Ask about assessments and documents needed.
- Consider sixth form routes if relevant. See [sixth form in Dubai](/guides/sixth-form-in-dubai).
