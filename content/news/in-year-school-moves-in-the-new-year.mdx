Children who move house during the school year apply for an **in-year place**. The process is different from the main round.

<Callout title="At a glance">

- Apply through your **local authority** or directly to some schools, depending on the area.
- **Waiting lists** may apply for popular schools.
- Start as soon as you know your **new address**.

</Callout>

## How it works

In-year applications are handled by the local authority or by individual schools, depending on the area and school type. Ask the council where to apply.

## What to do

- Check the council's process and the schools' availability.
- Apply for several schools if allowed.
- Ask about waiting lists.

## If refused

You can usually appeal. See [in-year school admissions](/guides/in-year-school-admissions-moving-mid-year), [how to change schools](/guides/how-to-change-schools-in-england) and [school admissions in England](/guides/school-admissions-england).
