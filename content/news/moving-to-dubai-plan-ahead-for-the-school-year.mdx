Many families move to Dubai for the start of the new school year, and popular British-curriculum schools can fill quickly. Starting your search months ahead gives you the best chance.

<Callout title="At a glance">

- **Register early** with several schools.
- Expect **assessments and documents**.
- Keep **backup options**.

</Callout>

## What to do now

- Shortlist schools using curriculum, location, fees and inspection results. See [KHDA ratings explained](/guides/khda-ratings-explained).
- Join waiting lists.
- Gather transfer documents from your current school.

See [Dubai school admissions: a practical timeline](/guides/dubai-school-admissions-timeline) and [British curriculum schools in Dubai](/guides/british-curriculum-schools-in-dubai-explained).
