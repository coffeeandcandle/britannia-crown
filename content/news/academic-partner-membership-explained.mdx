Our Academic Partner Membership gives families a monthly plan for online tutoring with consultant support. There is no long-term contract, and plans can be changed or cancelled from the family portal or by contacting us.

<Callout title="At a glance">

- **Foundation:** 4 hours of online tutoring a month and a monthly check-in.
- **Partner:** 8 hours a month with the same tutor at a fixed weekly time.
- **Full Partner:** 14 hours a month across up to two subjects.

</Callout>

## Choosing a plan

Weekly lessons suit many families, which often points to the Partner plan, while Foundation suits lighter support and Full Partner suits intensive needs or more than one subject. See [how often a child should have tutoring](/guides/how-often-should-a-child-have-tutoring).
