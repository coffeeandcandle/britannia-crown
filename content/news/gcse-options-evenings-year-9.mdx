Many schools hold **GCSE options evenings in Year 9**, though some do so in Year 8. They are a chance to ask the questions that matter.

<Callout title="At a glance">

- Check **which subjects are compulsory**.
- Ask how **options are chosen** and when changes are allowed.
- Think about **later pathways**, such as sixth form and careers.

</Callout>

## Questions to ask

- Which subjects are compulsory, and how many options do pupils pick?
- Do pupils need particular subjects for the school's sixth form?
- Can options be changed early in Year 10?
- What are the courses and exam boards like?

## Prepare with your child

Talk about what they enjoy and are good at, and which subjects might be needed later. See [how to choose GCSE options](/guides/how-to-choose-gcse-options) and [A-Level subject choices](/guides/a-level-subject-choices-and-university).

## Avoid common mistakes

Choose subjects for your child, not their friends, and keep options open. Read [how many GCSEs a child needs](/guides/how-many-gcses-does-a-child-need).
