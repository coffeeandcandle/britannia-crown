The 11-plus follows a **typical sequence**, though dates vary by area and school. Check each school's own timetable.

<Callout title="At a glance">

- **Registration** often opens in the summer before Year 6.
- **Tests** are commonly in the autumn.
- **Offers** follow on national offer day, 1 March.

</Callout>

## A typical timeline

1. **Summer before Year 6:** register for the test.
2. **Early autumn:** sit the test.
3. **Autumn:** receive results, sometimes with a pass mark or ranking.
4. **By 31 October:** submit your state school application through the local authority.
5. **1 March:** offers are released.

## What to check

Ask your target school for its test provider, registration deadline, test date and how results are used. See [the 11-plus and grammar schools](/guides/11-plus-and-grammar-schools-explained) and [standardised scores](/guides/11-plus-standardised-scores-explained).
