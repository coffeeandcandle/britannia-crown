Year 13 mocks matter because they influence **predicted grades** and show what to improve before the final exams.

<Callout title="At a glance">

- Mocks are **practice**, but they feed into teacher predictions.
- Review each paper to find **where marks were lost**.
- Build the **final months of revision** around those gaps.

</Callout>

## Why they matter

Teachers often use mocks to inform predicted grades. See [predicted grades explained](/guides/predicted-grades-explained).

## After the mocks

- Get marked papers and mark schemes.
- Sort lost marks into knowledge, technique and timing.
- Plan revision around the biggest gaps.
