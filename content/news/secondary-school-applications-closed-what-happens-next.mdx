The national closing date for secondary school applications in England for September 2027 entry was 31 October 2026. If you applied on time, your application is now with your local authority.

<Callout title="At a glance">

- On-time applications are **considered together** on offer day.
- Offers are sent on **1 March 2027**, the national offer day.
- If you missed the deadline, **apply now**. Late applications are usually handled after on-time ones.

</Callout>

## What happens now

Councils collect applications, check them against each school's admission criteria and allocate places. Nothing is decided by the order in which applications arrive, so there is nothing to do now but wait.

## If you missed the deadline

Contact your local authority straight away. Late applications may be considered after those received on time, so apply as soon as possible. Check your council's rules about late applications and reasons for lateness.

## Preparing for offer day

- Read the admission policies of your listed schools again.
- Keep notes about distance and criteria.
- Plan what you will do if you are not offered your first choice. See [what if you don't get your first choice school](/guides/what-if-you-dont-get-your-first-choice-school).

## Supporting your child

Keep conversations calm and positive about wherever they go. Many children thrive at schools their families did not rank first.
