Home-educated students who want GCSEs need to **plan well ahead**. Exams are sat as a private candidate through an exam centre.

<Callout title="At a glance">

- Start planning **18 to 24 months** before the exams.
- Find an **exam centre** that accepts private candidates.
- Check **entry deadlines**, which fall well before the exams.

</Callout>

## What to arrange

- Choose subjects and **exam boards**.
- Find a **centre** and ask about fees and deadlines.
- Check subjects with **practical elements**, which can be harder to take privately.
