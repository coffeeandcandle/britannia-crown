Two short statutory checks take place in primary schools in June each year: the phonics screening check in Year 1 and the multiplication tables check in Year 4.

<Callout title="At a glance">

- **Year 1:** reading real and made-up words aloud.
- **Year 4:** answering times tables questions on a screen.
- Both are **short** and low key; they help schools target support.

</Callout>

## What families can do

- Read together every day for Year 1 children. See [the Year 1 phonics screening check](/guides/year-1-phonics-screening-check-explained).
- Practise tables for Year 4 children in short bursts. See [the Year 4 multiplication tables check](/guides/multiplication-tables-check-year-4).
- Keep a relaxed tone.

## If your child finds them hard

Speak to the class teacher. Support in school can help children catch up. See [how to learn times tables](/guides/how-to-learn-times-tables).
