If you are moving to Dubai, you will hear about KHDA ratings. They are useful, but should be read in context.

<Callout title="In a nutshell">

- KHDA **regulates private schools** in Dubai and publishes inspection ratings.
- Ratings reflect **quality at inspection**.
- Always combine them with **visits and your own priorities**.

</Callout>

## What KHDA does

KHDA oversees private education in Dubai. It inspects schools, publishes reports and sets requirements on matters such as fees and standards. The inspections are carried out by a dedicated inspection body.

## How ratings work

Schools receive an overall rating on a scale from top to bottom, with several levels in between. The rating reflects areas such as student achievement, personal development, teaching and leadership. Look at the school's most recent report for specific findings.

## Using ratings

- Check the **date** of the report.
- Read the **detail**, including strengths and areas for improvement.
- Compare schools with similar curricula.

## What ratings leave out

Ratings do not show how happy your child will be, how a school handles particular needs, or whether it suits your family's budget and location. They also do not replace a visit.

## Comparison with England

If you are used to Ofsted, KHDA ratings play a similar role, although the systems are different. See [Ofsted ratings explained](/guides/ofsted-ratings-explained).

## Practical tips

- Look at several schools, not just the top-rated.
- Ask about waiting lists, since some highly rated schools fill quickly.
- Speak to current parents if you can.
