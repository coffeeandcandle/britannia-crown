Some independent schools admit children at 7 or 8 after an assessment. Because the children are young, schools often combine written tasks with observation and conversation.

<Callout title="In a nutshell">

- Assessments usually take place **in the autumn or winter** before entry. Check each school's dates.
- Expect **English, maths and informal activities**.
- Prepare **gently** and focus on confidence.

</Callout>

## What happens

Children may attend an assessment day where they do short papers and group activities, while staff watch how they communicate and cooperate. Some schools hold individual interviews.

## What is tested

- **English:** reading comprehension, writing and sometimes spelling.
- **Maths:** number, problem solving and basic arithmetic.
- **Reasoning:** at some schools, verbal or non-verbal puzzles.

## Registration and timing

Schools often ask for registration well before the assessment. Deadlines vary, so check each school's website and contact the admissions office.

## Preparing

- Read together daily.
- Practise number facts through games.
- Talk about the day so it is not a surprise.
- Avoid heavy drilling. Young children can lose confidence quickly.

## After the assessment

Schools may offer, waitlist or decline. If you are not offered a place, other routes may exist, including later entry points. Keep options open.
