Choosing a secondary school is a big decision. A structured approach helps you compare schools fairly and keep your child's needs at the centre.

<Callout title="In a nutshell">

- Start with your **child's needs** and your realistic options.
- Compare **progress, behaviour, curriculum and support**.
- **Visit** and ask questions.

</Callout>

## Step 1: Know your options

Your local authority publishes a booklet or website listing state schools and the admission rules. Include schools that may be a little further away, and consider whether any selective, faith or independent schools are options. See [school admissions in England](/guides/school-admissions-england).

## Step 2: Decide what matters

Think about your child's personality, interests and any additional needs. Consider size, ethos, subjects, sport, arts, languages, pastoral care and travel.

## Step 3: Look at the evidence

- **Results and progress:** see [Progress 8 and Attainment 8 explained](/guides/progress-8-and-attainment-8-explained).
- **Inspection reports:** see [Ofsted ratings explained](/guides/ofsted-ratings-explained).
- **Prospectus and website:** read them but remember they are marketing.
- **Parents' views:** ask local families.

## Step 4: Visit

Open evenings and tours show the atmosphere. Notice how pupils behave and speak to staff. Go back with your child if you can. See [questions to ask when visiting a school](/guides/questions-to-ask-when-visiting-a-school).

## Step 5: Be realistic

Look at how places were offered last year to judge your chances, and include at least one school you are confident of getting. See [school catchment areas explained](/guides/school-catchment-areas-explained).

## Step 6: Apply on time

Apply through your home local authority by the national deadline at the end of October in Year 6. Late applications may be treated less favourably.

## After offers

If you do not get your first choice, you have options. See [what if you don't get your first choice school](/guides/what-if-you-dont-get-your-first-choice-school).
