Parents often ask how many GCSEs their child should take. The answer depends on the school, the child and what they plan to do next.

<Callout title="In a nutshell">

- There is **no legal minimum** number of GCSEs.
- Most students take around **nine**, including English, maths and science.
- Sixth forms and colleges set **their own entry requirements**.

</Callout>

## What schools usually offer

Most schools expect students to take English Language, English Literature, maths and science (either combined or separate), plus a mix of other subjects. Many students take around nine GCSEs in total, though some take more or fewer.

## What is compulsory

In England, students must study English, maths and science, along with core subjects such as PE and RE and, in some cases, other subjects the school chooses. Not all of these lead to a GCSE for every student.

## What sixth forms and colleges want

Typical entry requirements include:

- a set number of GCSEs, often **five or more**, at grade 4 or 5;
- grades in English and maths, usually at least a 4;
- higher grades, such as a 6 or 7, in subjects students plan to study at A-Level.

Always check the requirements of the places your child might apply to. See [how to choose a sixth form or college](/guides/how-to-choose-a-sixth-form-or-college).

## If English and maths are not at grade 4

Students in England who do not reach grade 4 in English or maths generally need to continue studying them after 16. See [GCSE resits in English and maths](/guides/gcse-resits-english-and-maths).

## Fewer GCSEs

Some students take fewer GCSEs because of health, learning needs or school circumstances. This can be perfectly sensible, as long as they still have a route to what they want to do next. Talk to the school and to the post-16 providers early.

## Quality over quantity

Good grades in a reasonable number of subjects usually matter more than a large number of weaker results. Help your child pick subjects they can do well in. See [how to choose GCSE options](/guides/how-to-choose-gcse-options).
