Many UK independent schools offer **scholarships** for pupils with strong ability in a particular area. They are different from **bursaries**, which are financial help based on a family's means.

<Callout title="In a nutshell">

- Scholarships reward **ability**, such as academic, music, sport or art.
- Bursaries are **means-tested** help with fees.
- Rules and values **differ by school**, so read each school's policy.

</Callout>

## Types of scholarship

Common types include academic, music, art, drama and sport. Some schools offer all-rounder awards. Awards are usually a reduction in the fees, and the size varies.

## How to apply

Schools set their own process, which often includes an entrance exam, an audition, a portfolio or a trial. Applications are made through the school, usually in the autumn before entry.

## Scholarships and bursaries together

A scholarship may be combined with a bursary if a family qualifies. Ask each school how its awards work together.

## What to ask

- What are the criteria and the value?
- Is the award for the whole of the child's time at the school?
- What expectations come with it, such as taking part in music or sport?
