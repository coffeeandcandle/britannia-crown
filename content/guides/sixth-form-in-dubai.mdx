Families in Dubai have a choice of sixth form routes. Many British-curriculum schools offer **A-Levels**, and some schools offer the **International Baccalaureate (IB) Diploma** instead.

<Callout title="In a nutshell">

- **A-Levels**: usually three subjects, studied in depth over two years.
- **IB Diploma**: six subjects plus core elements, a broader programme.
- Both are accepted for university, so choose by **fit and subject availability**.

</Callout>

## A-Levels

A-Levels suit students who know which subjects they want to specialise in. They are examined at the end of Year 13. See [A-Level subject choices](/guides/a-level-subject-choices-and-university).

## The IB Diploma

The IB spreads study across six subjects, includes an extended essay and other core elements, and suits students who want breadth.

## Choosing a school

Look at subject choice, results, university destinations and entry requirements. Many sixth forms ask for specific GCSE or IGCSE grades. Check the inspection ratings in [KHDA ratings explained](/guides/khda-ratings-explained).
