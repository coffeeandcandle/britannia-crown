Parents apply for secondary school in **Year 6**, the final year of primary. The closing date is **31 October**, and the place starts in Year 7 the following September.

<Callout title="In a nutshell">

- Apply in the **autumn of Year 6**.
- The closing date is **31 October**.
- Offers arrive on **1 March**, and school starts in **September**.

</Callout>

## A simple timeline

- **Spring and summer of Year 5:** begin looking at schools.
- **September to October of Year 6:** visit open evenings and apply.
- **31 October:** national closing date.
- **1 March:** offers are released.
- **September:** Year 7 begins.

## Before you apply

Read your local authority's admissions booklet, check each school's criteria and visit schools. Selective schools may need a separate test and registration earlier. See [the 11-plus and grammar schools](/guides/11-plus-and-grammar-schools-explained).
