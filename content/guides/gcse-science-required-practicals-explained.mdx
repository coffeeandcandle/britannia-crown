GCSE science courses include a set of **required practicals**, experiments every student should carry out. They are assessed through questions in the **written exams**, not coursework.

<Callout title="In a nutshell">

- Required practicals are **set by the exam board** and done in class.
- They are tested through **exam questions**, which may use unfamiliar contexts.
- Revise the **method, variables, results and conclusion** for each one.

</Callout>

## Why they matter

A large number of marks in the science papers relate to practical skills: planning an experiment, choosing equipment, spotting risks and interpreting data. Students who have done the practicals handle these questions better.

## What questions ask

- **Planning**: identify independent, dependent and control variables.
- **Method**: describe steps clearly and say why.
- **Results**: draw and read graphs and tables.
- **Evaluation**: suggest improvements and discuss accuracy.

## How to revise them

Make a one-page summary for each practical covering the aim, apparatus, method, variables, hazards and what the results show. Then practise past paper questions.
