Good writers talk before they write. The best help at home is to make writing **short, purposeful and encouraging**.

<Callout title="In a nutshell">

- **Talk it through first**: ideas come before sentences.
- Make a **quick plan**: beginning, middle and end.
- Give **one piece of feedback at a time**.

</Callout>

## Ideas that help

- **Talk first.** Ask your child to tell you the idea before writing it.
- **Plan briefly.** A few bullet points make the writing easier.
- **Read aloud.** Reading a piece back helps children hear mistakes.
- **Write for real reasons**, such as postcards, lists or stories for a sibling.
- **Praise the ideas** before the spelling.

## Feedback

Pick one or two things, such as capital letters or a stronger verb, and leave the rest. Too much red pen discourages children.
