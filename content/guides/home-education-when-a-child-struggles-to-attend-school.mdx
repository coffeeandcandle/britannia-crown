When a child finds it very hard to go to school, families are often exhausted and worried. This guide offers general information and cannot replace professional advice.

<Callout title="In a nutshell">

- Look for **the cause**, and seek support from the school and health professionals.
- **Home education** can help some children, but is not a cure.
- Get **independent advice** before deciding.

</Callout>

## Why attendance becomes hard

Reasons can include anxiety, bullying, unmet learning needs, sensory difficulties, family changes or health problems. Often several factors combine. Understanding the cause matters.

## First steps

- Talk to the school: class teacher, SENCO and pastoral lead.
- Ask what adjustments, such as a reduced timetable or a safe space, can be tried.
- See your GP if you are concerned about your child's mental health.
- Ask about assessments for possible needs. See [SEN support and EHCPs explained](/guides/sen-support-and-ehcps-explained).

## What home education can offer

For some children, a calmer pace, flexibility and one-to-one attention help them recover confidence. It may allow learning to continue while wellbeing improves.

## What it cannot do alone

It does not remove underlying anxiety, and families may become isolated. It also places a lot on parents. Professional support, social contact and a plan for the future are important.

## Making the decision

Talk to others who have been through it, and seek independent advice. Think about what is best for your child over the long term, and whether you wish to keep the door open to returning to school. See [home education and the law](/guides/home-education-legal-requirements-england).

## Keeping learning going

Gentle routines, interests and short sessions can keep learning going. A kind tutor can help rebuild confidence. See [how to motivate a reluctant learner](/guides/how-to-motivate-a-reluctant-learner).

## If your child is in crisis

Seek urgent help from your GP or local emergency services if you are worried about your child's safety.
