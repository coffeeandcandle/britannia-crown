School year groups in England are set by age on 1 September. The table below shows the usual age at the start of each year, with the key stage and the main tests or exams.

<Callout title="In a nutshell">

- Children move up a year each **September**, based on their date of birth.
- The ages below are **those at the start of the school year**.
- Key stages group the years: KS1, KS2, KS3, KS4 and KS5.

</Callout>

## Year groups and ages

|---|---|---|---|
| Reception | 4 | EYFS | First year of primary school |

| Year 2 | 6 | KS1 | Teacher assessments |
| Year 3 | 7 | KS2 | |
| Year 4 | 8 | KS2 | Multiplication tables check |
| Year 5 | 9 | KS2 | |
| Year 6 | 10 | KS2 | SATs in May |
| Year 7 | 11 | KS3 | Start of secondary school |
| Year 8 | 12 | KS3 | |

## What the key stages mean

Each key stage ends with an assessment in some form. Read our guides to [Key Stage 1 assessments](/guides/key-stage-1-assessments-explained), [KS2 SATs](/guides/ks2-sats-explained) and [Key Stage 3](/guides/key-stage-3-explained).
