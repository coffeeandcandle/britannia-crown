A-Level content is demanding, so a tutor needs **real subject depth** as well as the ability to explain clearly.

<Callout title="In a nutshell">

- Look for **strong subject knowledge**, ideally to degree level, and teaching skill.
- Check **exam board** experience and familiarity with recent papers.
- Agree **goals and frequency** early, and review after a few weeks.

</Callout>

## What to look for

- **Depth in the subject**, so the tutor can handle the hardest questions.
- **Exam board and paper knowledge**, including question styles and mark schemes.
- **A track record** with students at a similar level.
- **A plan** that fits your child's goals and timetable.

## Questions to ask

- What is your background in the subject?
- Which boards and papers have you worked with?
- How will you adapt to my child's gaps?
- How will we measure progress?

## When to start and how often

Starting early in Year 12 is ideal. Weekly sessions suit many students. See [how often a child should have tutoring](/guides/how-often-should-a-child-have-tutoring) and [when to stop](/guides/when-to-stop-tutoring).
