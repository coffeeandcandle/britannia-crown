A child who does well at school can still benefit from tutoring. The aim is different: stretch, depth and preparation for a particular goal rather than catching up.

<Callout title="In a nutshell">

- Tutoring for high achievers focuses on **depth, stretch and specific goals**.
- Keep it **interesting and proportionate**. The aim is enjoyment as well as results.
- Agree the goal with your child first.

</Callout>

## Why a strong student might want a tutor

- They are **bored** in class and want a challenge.
- They are aiming for a **selective school, scholarship or competitive course**.
- They want a **top grade**, such as 8 or 9 at GCSE or an A* at A-Level, and need exam technique to match their understanding.
- They love a subject and want to **go further** than the syllabus.

## What good stretch looks like

A tutor can introduce topics beyond the curriculum, harder problem-solving, wider reading and discussion. For example, a maths tutor might use competition-style problems, while an English tutor might explore literary criticism or creative writing techniques.

## Preparing for a particular goal

Entrance tests, scholarship papers and university admissions tests all have their own style. Focused preparation, for example for the [11-plus](/guides/11-plus-and-grammar-schools-explained) or for independent school entry, can help a strong child perform to their potential. See [independent school entry assessments and bursaries](/guides/independent-school-entry-assessments-and-bursaries).

## Avoiding pressure

High achievers can be hard on themselves. Warning signs include perfectionism, trouble sleeping or loss of enjoyment. Keep a balance between work and rest, and let your child have a say in what they study. If you are worried about stress, read [how to support a child through exam stress](/guides/how-to-support-a-child-through-exam-stress).

## Choosing the tutor

Look for subject expertise, not just general experience. For stretch work, a tutor who has studied the subject to a high level, or taught the relevant exam board, can make a real difference.
