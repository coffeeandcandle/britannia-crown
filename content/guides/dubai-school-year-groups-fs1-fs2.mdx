British-curriculum schools in Dubai use the same year names as England, with early years labelled **FS1 and FS2** (Foundation Stage). They match Nursery and Reception.

<Callout title="In a nutshell">

- **FS1** is for three-year-olds, similar to Nursery.
- **FS2** is for four-year-olds, similar to Reception.
- **Year 1** follows at five, then the school year numbers continue as in England.

</Callout>

## How they line up

| Dubai British school | Usual age | England equivalent |
|---|---|---|
| FS1 | 3 | Nursery |
| FS2 | 4 | Reception |
| Year 1 | 5 | Year 1 |
| Year 7 | 11 | Year 7 |

The usual age is the age by the school's cut-off date, which is commonly around 31 August but differs by school and curriculum.

## Why dates matter

A child's year may depend on the cut-off date used by the school, so a child could be in a different year to their English peers. Ask each school how it places children.
