If your child misses a GCSE exam, act quickly. **Contact the school or exam centre straight away**, because only they can talk to the exam board.

<Callout title="In a nutshell">

- **Tell the centre immediately**, with evidence if the reason is illness.
- **Special consideration** may apply if your child was ill or affected during the exam.
- A **resit** is usually the main route, in the next series.

</Callout>

## What to do on the day

If your child is ill or something goes wrong, telephone the school before the exam if you can. Keep any medical or other evidence, as the centre may need it.

## Special consideration

If a student sits the exam but was affected by illness, bereavement or another event at the time, the centre may apply for special consideration. It results in a small adjustment to marks, and the rules are set by the exam boards.

## If the exam is missed completely

Options depend on the board, the reason and how much of the qualification remains. The centre will explain whether a late sitting is possible or whether the next step is a resit. See [GCSE resits](/guides/gcse-resits-english-and-maths).

## Looking after your child

Missing an exam is stressful. Reassure your child and focus on the next step. See [supporting a child through exam stress](/guides/how-to-support-a-child-through-exam-stress) and [what to do on exam day](/guides/what-to-do-on-exam-day).
