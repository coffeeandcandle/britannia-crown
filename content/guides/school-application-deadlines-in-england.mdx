State school applications in England follow **national closing dates**. Your local authority runs the process, and the dates are the same everywhere.

<Callout title="In a nutshell">

- **Secondary (Year 7):** applications close on **31 October**.
- **Primary (Reception):** applications close on **15 January**.
- **Offer days** are 1 March for secondary and 16 April for primary, or the next working day if that falls on a weekend.

</Callout>

## The dates

| Stage | Applications open | Closing date | Offers |
|---|---|---|---|
| Secondary (Year 7) | September | 31 October | 1 March |
| Primary (Reception) | September | 15 January | 16 April |

Applications are made through your local authority, even for schools in another area. Check your local authority's website for how to apply.

## Independent and sixth form

Independent schools set their own registration dates, which may be years ahead. Sixth forms and colleges set their own dates, often in the autumn. See [independent school entry assessments](/guides/independent-school-entry-assessments-and-bursaries).

## If you miss a deadline

Apply as soon as you can. Late applications are usually considered after on-time ones. See [late applications](/guides/late-school-applications-in-england).
