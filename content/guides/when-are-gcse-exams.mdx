Main GCSE exams in England take place in the **summer term, from about May to June**. Exact dates are set each year by the exam boards.

<Callout title="In a nutshell">

- Main series: **May to June**.
- Results: **August**.
- English language and maths also have a **November** series for resits.

</Callout>

## How the timetable works

Each exam board publishes its timetable well in advance. Students may have more than one exam on some days and gaps on others, depending on their subjects. Schools share the final timetable, so use it rather than a general date.

## Mocks

Many schools run mock exams in the autumn or winter of Year 11. See [how to use mock exams](/guides/gcse-mock-exams-how-to-use-them).

## Results

Results are released in August, normally on a Thursday, and sixth forms confirm places that day. See [results day: what to do](/guides/results-day-gcse-a-level-what-to-do).

## Resits and private candidates

English language and maths can be retaken in November, and other subjects in the next summer series. Private candidates enter through an exam centre, and deadlines fall well before the exams. See [GCSE resits](/guides/gcse-resits-english-and-maths) and [entering GCSEs as a private candidate](/guides/private-candidate-gcse-how-to-enter).

Check your exam board's website for the dates for the current year, as they change.
