Choosing a primary school is often parents' first big education decision. A little preparation helps you find a school where your child can flourish.

<Callout title="In a nutshell">

- Think about **atmosphere, teaching and fit** for your child.
- **Visit** several schools, and ask questions.
- Apply by **15 January** for Reception.

</Callout>

## What to consider

- **Atmosphere:** do children seem happy, engaged and well looked after?
- **Teaching:** how do they teach reading, maths and writing?
- **Early years:** how do they settle new children and use play?
- **Wellbeing and behaviour:** how do they support children's needs?
- **Practicalities:** location, wraparound care, school hours and costs.

## Looking at information

Read the latest inspection report, see the website and check how places were offered in previous years. Reports and results are one piece of evidence. See [Ofsted ratings explained](/guides/ofsted-ratings-explained).

## Visits

Go on tours or open days, and if you can, see classrooms in action. Ask about class sizes, how reading is taught, communication with parents and how children are supported if they struggle. See [questions to ask when visiting a school](/guides/questions-to-ask-when-visiting-a-school).

## Understanding admissions

Admission rules vary. Many schools use distance, siblings and other criteria. Read the policy for each school you like. See [school catchment areas explained](/guides/school-catchment-areas-explained).

## Applying

Apply through your home local authority. List your preferences in order, including at least one school you are likely to get. The national closing date is 15 January for September starts, with offers on 16 April, or the next working day.
