Securing a school place in Dubai can take time, especially at popular schools. A clear timeline helps.

<Callout title="In a nutshell">

- **Start early** and register with several schools.
- Expect **assessments, interviews and documents**.
- Keep **backup options**.

</Callout>

## Several months before

- Research schools and curricula.
- Check fee structures and location.
- Register interest and join waiting lists.

## Two to three months before

- Submit formal applications.
- Gather documents: passports, previous reports and any assessments.
- Book virtual tours or visits.

## Before you arrive

- Complete any online assessments or interviews.
- Respond promptly to offers, since some expire quickly.
- Pay any deposits as required, noting refund terms.

## After arrival

- Visit schools in person if you have not.
- Follow up on waiting lists.
- Consider a temporary option while you wait.

## Documents

Schools often ask for transfer documents from the previous school and school reports. Ask your current school early, and keep copies of exam results. See [returning to the UK with children](/guides/returning-to-the-uk-with-children-school-places) for the reverse journey.

## Age and entry

Year groups and age cut-offs are set by each school within local rules. Check with each school how your child would be placed.

## Costs

Ask for a complete list of fees, including registration, deposits, uniform, transport and extras.
