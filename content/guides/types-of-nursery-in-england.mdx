There are several types of early years provider in England. They differ in who runs them, the hours they offer and how much they cost.

<Callout title="In a nutshell">

- **Nursery schools and classes** are state-funded and teacher-led.
- **Private day nurseries** are run privately, often with long hours.
- **Pre-schools** and **childminders** offer smaller or home-based settings.

</Callout>

## Maintained nursery schools and classes

State nursery schools, and nursery classes attached to primary schools, are led or supported by qualified teachers. Places are often part-time, and priority may be given to some groups. Nursery classes can feed into Reception at the same school, but a place does not guarantee one.

## Private day nurseries

Run by companies or individuals, day nurseries often open for long hours and take babies and young children. Fees vary, and many offer government-funded hours.

## Pre-schools and playgroups

These are often run by community groups and operate during school hours. They are suited to part-time sessions.

## Childminders

Childminders look after small groups in their own homes. They can be flexible and suit families who want a home setting.
