An education consultant helps families make decisions about schools, learning and qualifications. The work varies, but there are common themes.

<Callout title="In a nutshell">

- Consultants advise on **schools, applications, tutoring and exams**.
- They are most useful when families are **moving, short of time or choosing between routes**.
- Always ask **how they charge** and whether they are independent of the schools they suggest.

</Callout>

## What they help with

- **School search and shortlisting**, based on location, curriculum, fees and fit.
- **Applications and admissions**, including deadlines, tests and interviews.
- **Tutoring and academic planning**, matching a child with support.
- **Qualifications**, such as GCSE and A-Level choices and private candidate entries.
- **Relocation**, for families moving to or from the UK.

## When it helps

Families moving between countries, those applying to selective or oversubscribed schools, and those unsure which route suits a child often find advice useful. A consultant can save time and help avoid missed deadlines.

## What to ask

- What experience do you have with my situation?
- How do you charge, and what is included?
- Do you receive payments from schools?
- Can I speak to previous clients?
