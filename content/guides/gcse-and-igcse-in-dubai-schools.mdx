Many British-curriculum schools in Dubai teach **International GCSEs (IGCSEs)** rather than GCSEs. They are separate qualifications at the same level.

<Callout title="In a nutshell">

- IGCSEs are the **international version** of GCSEs and are common abroad.
- Both are taken at about **age 16** and are widely accepted for sixth form and university.
- Moving between them partway through is **hard**, so plan early.

</Callout>

## Why IGCSEs in Dubai

IGCSEs are designed for students around the world and are standard in many British schools abroad. State schools in England now mostly teach GCSEs, so families moving between the two may meet both. See [IGCSE vs GCSE](/guides/igcse-vs-gcse).

## Grading

IGCSEs from some boards use the 9 to 1 scale, others use letter grades. Check which your child's course uses.

## Moving countries

A child moving between schools in Years 10 or 11 may find the courses differ. Ask the new school what it offers and whether it can accommodate the child. See [sitting UK exams abroad](/guides/sitting-uk-exams-abroad).
