Exam days are easier when the practicalities are sorted. This checklist helps students and parents feel calm and prepared.

<Callout title="In a nutshell">

- **Check dates, times and equipment** the day before.
- Eat, sleep and **arrive early**.
- If something goes wrong, **tell the school quickly**.

</Callout>

## The day before

- Confirm the exam time, venue and subject.
- Pack a **clear pencil case**: black pens, pencils, ruler, calculator with spare batteries if allowed.
- Lay out uniform or clothes, and plan the journey.
- Have a light revision session, then rest. Cramming late rarely helps.

## The morning

- Eat a proper breakfast.
- Leave early to avoid stress about transport.
- Keep conversation positive and brief.
- Bring ID if the school requires it.

## In the exam room

- Phones, smartwatches and notes must be handed in or switched off and stored as instructed. Breaking the rules can lead to disqualification.
- Read instructions and each question carefully.
- Plan time per question and keep an eye on the clock.
- Leave time at the end to check.

## After the exam

Avoid long post-mortems. Encourage a break and a snack, then focus on the next exam. Mistakes cannot be changed, and talking them over can increase worry.

## If something goes wrong

Illness, a bereavement, a clash of dates or a problem on the day should be reported to the school as soon as possible. Special consideration may be available in some cases. See [exam access arrangements](/guides/exam-access-arrangements-extra-time).

## Looking after your child

Keep routines calm. Encourage sleep and exercise. See [how to support a child through exam stress](/guides/how-to-support-a-child-through-exam-stress).

<Cta>

If your child feels unprepared, talk to us about focused support.

</Cta>
