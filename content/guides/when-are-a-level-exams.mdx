A-Level exams in England take place in the **summer term, from about May to June**, at the end of Year 13. Results are released in August.

<Callout title="In a nutshell">

- Exams: **May to June**.
- Results: **August**, which is also when university places are confirmed.
- Retakes are usually in the following **summer**.

</Callout>

## How the timetable works

Exam boards publish their timetables in advance, and schools or colleges tell students which papers fall on which days. The A-Level subjects a student takes decide the number of papers, and the dates can vary by board.

## Before the exams

Students often sit mock exams in the winter or spring of Year 13, and teachers use these to inform predicted grades. See [predicted grades explained](/guides/predicted-grades-explained).

## Results and what follows

Results come out in August. Universities then confirm offers, and students who miss grades may go through Clearing. See [results day: what to do](/guides/results-day-gcse-a-level-what-to-do).

## Retaking

Students can retake A-Levels, and many do so the following summer. See [retaking A-Levels](/guides/retaking-a-levels).

## Preparing

Practising with past papers is the most useful preparation. Read [how to use past papers](/guides/how-to-use-past-papers). Check the exam board's website for the dates for the current year.
