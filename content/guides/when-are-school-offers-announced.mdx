Offers for state school places in England are released on **national offer days**. You receive your offer from your local authority, not directly from the school.

<Callout title="In a nutshell">

- **Secondary offers:** 1 March.
- **Primary offers:** 16 April.
- If the date falls on a weekend, offers are sent the **next working day**.

</Callout>

## What happens on the day

Parents who applied online receive an email, and many local authorities also publish offers in an online account. Offers are sent the same day, often early in the morning.

## What if you did not get your first choice?

You will be offered a place at the highest-ranked school on your list that your child qualifies for. You can accept it, join the waiting list for higher preferences and, if you wish, appeal. Read [what if you don't get your first choice](/guides/what-if-you-dont-get-your-first-choice-school).

## Accepting the offer

Respond by the date given. Accepting a place does not stop you appealing for another, so secure a place first.

## Appeals

You normally have a set period to lodge an appeal after the offer. See [school admission appeals](/guides/school-admission-appeals-england).
