If you miss the closing date for school applications, apply **as soon as possible**. Local authorities still accept late applications, but they are usually processed after on-time ones.

<Callout title="In a nutshell">

- Late applications are usually considered **after all on-time ones**.
- **Good reasons**, such as moving into the area, can be treated as on time.
- Apply straight away and keep **evidence** for the reason.

</Callout>

## What happens to a late application

Local authorities process applications received by the deadline first. Late ones are considered once those places have been allocated, which can mean the most popular schools are full.

## When late is treated as on time

Some authorities accept a late application as on time if there is a good reason, such as a family moving into the area close to the deadline, or serious illness. Ask your authority what evidence it needs.

## What to do

1. Contact the local authority and ask how to apply.
2. Submit the application as soon as possible.
3. List several realistic preferences, including your catchment school.
4. Ask to go on waiting lists for schools you prefer.

## If you are moving during the year

Different rules apply to children who join mid-year. See [in-year admissions](/guides/in-year-school-admissions-moving-mid-year) and [school admissions in England](/guides/school-admissions-england).
