Adults can retake GCSEs at any age. English language and maths are the most common, because many jobs, courses and apprenticeships ask for a **grade 4**.

<Callout title="In a nutshell">

- There is **no age limit** for retaking GCSEs.
- Options are a **local college**, an **evening class**, or entering as a **private candidate**.
- Adults without a 4 in English or maths may qualify for **funded study**. Check with the college.

</Callout>

## Where to study

- **Colleges and adult learning centres** run GCSE English and maths courses, often in the evening, and enter students for the exams.
- **Private study** with a tutor or online course, then entering as a private candidate through an exam centre.

## Entering the exams

The main exams are in the summer, and English language and maths also run in November. Entry deadlines are set by the exam centre and fall well before the exams. See [entering GCSEs as a private candidate](/guides/private-candidate-gcse-how-to-enter) and [when are GCSE exams](/guides/when-are-gcse-exams).

## How long does it take?

It depends on the starting point and the time available. Many adults prepare over a few months to a year, with regular practice and feedback.
