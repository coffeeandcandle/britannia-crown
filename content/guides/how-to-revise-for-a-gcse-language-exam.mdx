Modern language GCSEs test four skills: listening, speaking, reading and writing. Revision should cover them all.

<Callout title="In a nutshell">

- Practise **all four skills** each week.
- Learn **vocabulary little and often**.
- Practise **speaking out loud**.

</Callout>

## Know what is tested

Check the exam board and the topics, and the weighting between skills. Speaking and listening are as important as reading and writing.

## Vocabulary

Use flashcards, apps or spaced repetition. Learn words in groups by topic, and practise using them in sentences. Revisit old words often.

## Grammar

Focus on key tenses, common irregular verbs, agreements and sentence structures. Practise writing short paragraphs that use them correctly.

## Listening

Use past paper recordings, podcasts and videos. Listen first for gist, then for detail. Replay and check with a transcript.

## Speaking

Practise aloud, record yourself and listen back. Prepare answers to likely questions with opinions and reasons. A tutor or language partner can give feedback.

## Reading and writing

Read short texts regularly, and note useful phrases. For writing, learn a bank of structures to use and check for accuracy.

## Using past papers

Use official papers to practise timing and question types. See [how to use past papers](/guides/how-to-use-past-papers).

## Little and often

Short daily sessions work better than long weekly ones. See [how to build a revision timetable](/guides/how-to-build-a-revision-timetable).
