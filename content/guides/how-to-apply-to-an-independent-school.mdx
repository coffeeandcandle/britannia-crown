Independent schools run their own admissions, so the process differs from state schools. The steps below are the usual path.

<Callout title="In a nutshell">

- **Register early.** Some schools take registrations years ahead.
- Expect **assessments and an interview**, with format depending on age and school.
- Each school sets its own **dates, fees and process**.

</Callout>

## Step by step

1. **Research.** Look at schools' websites and attend open days.
2. **Visit.** Book a tour and ask about entry points.
3. **Register.** Complete the school's registration form and pay any fee.
4. **Assessments.** Children usually sit tests and may attend an interview or group activity.
5. **Offer.** Schools send offers, often with a deadline to accept and a deposit.

## Entry points

Common entry points are 4, 7, 8, 11, 13 and 16, depending on the school. See [7+ and 8+ entry](/guides/seven-plus-and-eight-plus-entry) and [Common Entrance and prep schools](/guides/common-entrance-and-prep-schools-explained).

## Costs and help

Fees vary widely, and some schools offer scholarships and bursaries. See [independent school scholarships](/guides/independent-school-scholarships-explained).

## Preparing your child

Keep preparation relaxed and focus on reading and number. Read [entry assessments and bursaries](/guides/independent-school-entry-assessments-and-bursaries) and [independent or state school](/guides/independent-or-state-school).
