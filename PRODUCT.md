# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

British and international expat parents in Dubai and the GCC with children aged 0-18, seeking UK-curriculum education support: nursery search, British school placement, IGCSE/GCSE/A-Level tutoring, homeschool support, or full education concierge services. They are evaluating whether to trust Britannia Crown with a high-stakes, high-cost decision about their child's education, often while newly relocated and unfamiliar with the local school system (KHDA ratings, waitlists, curriculum differences).

Secondary audience: prospective "ambassadors" (nannies, teachers, community members) evaluating the referral programme.

## Product Purpose

A marketing/lead-generation site for Britannia Crown, a British education consultancy in Dubai. It exists to convert visiting parents into consultation bookings (via contact form, WhatsApp, or free guide downloads) and to recruit ambassadors into the referral programme. Success = qualified enquiries and ambassador sign-ups, not pageviews.

## Brand Personality

Trust, confidence, and quiet premium credibility — British heritage without stuffiness. The site should read as an established, expert partner (not a directory or generic tutoring marketplace) that expat families can hand a major decision to with confidence. Warm enough to feel personal (family-oriented, community-embedded via the ambassador programme) but never casual or gimmicky.

## Anti-references

No specific named anti-reference. Generally avoid: generic template/stock-agency feel, overly corporate/cold styling, and anything that reads as a discount tutoring marketplace rather than a boutique consultancy.

## Design Principles

- Expert confidence over hard selling — the copy and layout should establish authority (UK curriculum specialists, KHDA knowledge, vetted tutor network) before asking for the conversion.
- One relationship, full journey — reinforce "birth to A-Level, one partner" visually and structurally rather than presenting services as disconnected offerings.
- Premium restraint — burgundy/champagne British-heritage palette, serif display type for authority, generous whitespace; avoid visual clutter or trend-chasing effects.
- Multiple low-friction conversion paths — WhatsApp, email, and form should all feel equally easy; never force a single funnel.
- Trust-building through specificity — real numbers (144K students, 240K British nationals, 50+ tutors) over vague claims.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Standard best practices: sufficient color contrast, readable type sizes, keyboard navigation, semantic HTML, respect for reduced-motion preferences. No formal WCAG compliance mandate at this time.
